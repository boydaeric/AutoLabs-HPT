**Terms used in this log**

* **Decision.** *use* (support placement in the body), *revise* (the finding changes existing v16 text), *footnote* (a note in the named appendix; v16 has no footnotes and v2 adds none, so footnote-level material goes under the existing appendix bullets), *hold* (no placement in v2), *cut* (dropped, with the reason).
* **Placement.** Given by v16 section. "App." means a v16 appendix bullet (A to J).
* **Evidence rule.** Any finding or part of one whose status is unverifiable, not found, not checked or secondary only is hold-level and gets no lead or support placement. "Located" verifies what a commenter wrote, not the commenter's claims about anyone else. Corrected findings use the corrected fact.
* **Citation rule.** v2 adds no citation that the Citation check or the verification summary does not confirm, and uses the pinpoint they give. Every other proposed citation goes to the "citation to add after verification" list in `review/article_changes.md`. Existing v16 citations stay unless a verified source contradicts them (see decision 4).
* **Quote rule.** No new quotes from either Federal Register rule. v16 quotes CMS-2449-P once (91 FR 30453, one passage in two fragments, sec. 4) and CMS-2452-P twice (the Table 12 title and the "shown in table 12" wording, sec. 5), which is one more than CONVENTIONS section 5 allows. v2 keeps the Table 12 title and paraphrases the "shown in table 12" wording.
